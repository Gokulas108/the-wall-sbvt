### 1. What is the Birnagar Temple Project?

The Birnagar Temple Project is a spiritual and cultural initiative to build a temple dedicated to Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur near his sacred birthplace in Birnagar, West Bengal. The project aims to glorify his life, teachings, and legacy through a temple, museum, library, educational facilities, and spaces for pilgrims and visitors.

---

### 2. Why was this project started?

The project was started to honor Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur, one of the most influential acharyas in the Gaudiya Vaishnava tradition, and to fulfill the long-standing desire of Srila Prabhupada that a temple and memorial be established in Birnagar. The project will create a spiritual center where future generations can learn about Bhaktivinoda Thakur’s contributions and connect with his teachings.

---

### 3. Who is Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur?

Srila Bhaktivinoda Thakur (1838–1914), born as Kedarnath Datta in Birnagar, was a renowned scholar, magistrate, author, poet, and spiritual teacher. He revived and preserved the teachings of Sri Chaitanya Mahaprabhu, established the Nama Hatta preaching movement, rediscovered Lord Chaitanya’s birthplace in Mayapur, and laid the foundation for the worldwide Krishna consciousness movement that later spread through Srila Prabhupada. He is often honored as the “Pioneer of Modern Bhakti” and the “Seventh Goswami.”

### 5. What is the Wall of Legacy Campaign?

The Wall of Legacy is a special fundraising campaign inviting 100,000 devotees to participate in building the Birnagar Temple. Every donor who contributes ₹1,000 or more can have their name permanently inscribed on the Wall of Legacy. The campaign aims to bring together thousands of devotees so that the temple is built collectively by the global devotional community rather than a small group of donors.

---

### 6. How do I donate to the Wall of Legacy Campaign?

Donating is simple:

1. Visit the Wall of Legacy donation page.
2. Choose your contribution amount (minimum ₹1,000).
3. Select the wall block where you would like your name to appear.
4. Enter your details exactly as you want your name displayed.
5. Complete the payment using the available payment methods.
6. Your name will be reserved on the Wall of Legacy according to your contribution.

> **Note:** For every ₹1,000 donated, your name appears once on the wall. Larger contributions allow your name to appear multiple times.
